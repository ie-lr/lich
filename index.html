📱 Install App Icon on Android & iPhone

Install LITRICH as a native application on your phone with the couple logo icon and offline capability.

🤖

Android / Chrome / Edge

Click the "Install App" button in the header bar or menu to add the app directly to your phone screen.

🍏

iPhone / iPad (Safari)

Tap the Share button (box with upward arrow) at the bottom, then choose "Add to Home Screen".
